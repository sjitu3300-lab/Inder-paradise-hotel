Frequently Asked Questions

What types of rooms are available at Hotel Inder Paradise?
We offer Super Deluxe Suite Rooms, Deluxe Executive AC Rooms, and Standard AC Rooms equipped with smart TV, comfortable bedding, and attached modern washrooms.
What are the check-in and check-out timings?
Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Flexible check-in/check-out is available upon request depending on room availability.
Can we book the banquet hall for marriage and birthday parties?
Yes, our banquet hall is fully equipped for weddings, ring ceremonies, anniversary parties, birthday celebrations, and business conferences with customized catering.
How do I make a quick reservation?
You can instantly book by clicking on our WhatsApp button or calling our reception desk directly at +91 92570 90750.
